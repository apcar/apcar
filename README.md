# Andrew Greene

I build partnership operating models that turn ecosystems into measurable growth, drawing on experience across technology platforms and global services firms. Today, I lead global Ecosystems & Partnerships at Coforge across frontier AI labs, hyperscalers, data platforms, and enterprise software.

Alongside that work, I build software and research systems for questions I cannot leave alone. The subjects range from AI and markets to history, writing, memory, and everyday product problems. The recurring work is connecting research, judgment, execution, and evidence so the next effort can build on the last.

Most have a user base of one. I describe the machinery here, not the material that runs through it. These are independent projects, with their own scope and stages of development.

## Public work

- **[CouncilLogic](https://github.com/apcar/CouncilLogic) · Public alpha** — Compare independent model responses, examine their disagreements, and keep a record of how a conclusion was reached. The local-first Python engine combines blinded judging, deterministic aggregation, bounded execution, and restart recovery. Models contribute judgment; the person retains decision authority.

- **[PageParcel](https://github.com/apcar/PageParcel) · Browser extension** — Preserve a webpage so another person—or an agent—can inspect what was actually there. Packages screenshots, readable and structured Markdown, source context, and integrity hashes into one portable record, processed locally without uploading page contents.

## Private systems and pilots

- **TickerTape · Working research system** — Make unusual options activity something I can investigate. Reconstructs market observations into inspectable evidence, opens research cases, and tracks paper decisions and later outcomes. Observations, interpretations, and results remain distinct.

- **The Apcar Chronicle · Working research corpus** — Reconstruct a family's history across centuries and countries without losing the sources or filling the gaps with plausible stories. Connects people, events, places, and documents through an evidence graph, with research queues, dependency tracking, and publication views.

- **Research Edition Engine · Local AI research workbench** — Turn historical-language scans into traceable research editions. The Grabar (Classical Armenian) pilot combines custom-trained OCR, locally running small language models (SLMs), translation candidates, and a side-by-side review workbench. Model disagreement drives targeted escalation, with each reading linked to its source image.

- **SystemsLog · Persistent memory for people and agents** — Carry memory, context, and working state across systems, conversations, files, and agents. A private continuity system built around durable records, attributed contributions, and source-linked retrieval. Corrections enter as timestamped additions, letting context accumulate while preserving the original record.

- **Authoring Studio · Local AI authoring app** — Bring book structure, long-form writing, and agent-assisted editing into one Mac workspace. Combines a navigable manuscript map, continuous rich-text editing, structured manuscript import, recoverable revisions, and publication exports. AI editorial changes arrive as reviewable proposals, with the author controlling what enters the manuscript.

- **Impresa Foundry · Domain intelligence and venture discovery** — Index more than a million domain names, then use contextual retrieval, model-assisted screening, and tiered review to investigate promising names, project fits, and business concepts. Human feedback informs versioned review context, with model nominations and acquisition decisions kept distinct.

Supporting workflows connect research to build roadmaps, route document and presentation work, coordinate agents, and carry decisions between runs.
